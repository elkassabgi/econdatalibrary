Equities · live

HF Data Library

1-minute OHLCV bars
Research-grade 1-minute OHLCV bars for 1,391 U.S. stocks and ETFs back to 2002 — raw and cleaned versions, 25 pre-computed academic variables per ticker/day, updated every trading day. A free, citable TAQ/CRSP alternative.
hfdatalibrary.com →

Macro & finance · live

Econ Data Library

economic & financial series
Global economic & financial data: billions of individual series from the worlds statistical sources — national accounts, prices, trade, labor, energy, markets — in one namespace, with per-series licensing and producer-first citations, auto-updated.
econdatalibrary.com →

Patents & innovation · live

IP Data Library

9.4M+ patents · innovation measures
Research-grade innovation measures computed from the complete US patent record — citations, originality & generality, firm patent panels — free, citable, reproducible, with honest methodology. US patent data is public domain.
ipdatalibrary.com →
