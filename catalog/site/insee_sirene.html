Full description

INSEE SIRENE company registry full ingest. Downloads all active French legal units (siren) and establishments (siret). ~12M active legal units, ~30M establishments. License: Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0 — commercial redistribution OK. GDPR carve-out: skip records where statutDiffusion = 'N' (natural person opt-out). Attribution: Source: INSEE SIRENE (Licence Ouverte 2.0) www.insee.fr API: https://api.insee.fr/api-sirene/3.11 Auth: X-INSEE-Api-Key-Integration header Pagination: curseurSuivant cursor, 10k records/page Run: python jobs/ingest_insee_sirene.py [siren|siret|both]
