Econ Data Library

Econ Data Library is the largest totally free online database in the world — dedicated to bringing all of the world’s freely available data into a single, easily accessible location, with the help of cutting-edge AI tools. Built and maintained by Ahmed Elkassabgi at the University of Central Arkansas.
