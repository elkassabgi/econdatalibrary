How to Cite

Citations here are producer-first: credit the original statistical agency before the library. Every series and bundle ships its own ready-made citation.

Citing a series

Each series' citation (original producer, license, retrieval date, series id) is included in its CSV download header and its metadata.json. Use that citation — it names the agency that actually produced the numbers.

Citing the library

Elkassabgi, A. (2026). Economic Data Library: Free Economic and Financial Data (version 1.0) [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21405120

BibTeX

@dataset{econdatalibrary,
  author    = {Elkassabgi, Ahmed},
  title     = {{Economic Data Library: Free Economic and Financial Data}},
  year      = {2026},
  version   = {1.0},
  publisher = {Zenodo},
  doi       = {10.5281/zenodo.21405120},
  url       = {https://econdatalibrary.com}
}

Reproducibility note

For exact reproducibility, cite the bundle snapshot date shown in your download's manifest — the same snapshot always resolves to the same data.
