Reporting data issues

If you find an error in the data — wrong values, missing observations, a bad unit or label — please email me with:

  • The series id (e.g. worldbank:NY.GDP.MKTP.CD:USA)
  • Date(s) or observation(s) affected
  • Description of the issue
  • How you identified it (comparison source, expected value, etc.)

Every reported issue is investigated against the original publisher's data.

Requesting new sources or series

If you need a source or series that isn't included, email me the source, what you need from it, and the research use — sources are added in batches as licensing permits.
