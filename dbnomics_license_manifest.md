# DBnomics Provider License Audit — econdatalibrary.com

**Audit date:** 2026-06-10. **Scope:** all 79 providers in `dbnomics_license_manifest.json`.
**Context:** DBnomics states "Data distributed by DBnomics is subject to the same license and terms of use as its original source provider," so each provider's own terms govern re-hosting.

**Method & integrity notes.** Every classification traces to a page fetched (WebFetch) or a PDF downloaded and read in full during this audit. Quotes are verbatim from those pages/PDFs. Where a provider blocked direct fetching (403/timeout/connection refused), rows marked **"search snippet"** rely on search-engine excerpts of the named provider page; treat those five OPEN rows (BOE, CBO, METI, UNCTAD, UNIDO) and the snippet-based RESTRICTED/CONDITIONAL rows as high-likelihood but re-verify manually before launch. Nothing was quoted from memory. UNKNOWN rows list exactly what was tried.
